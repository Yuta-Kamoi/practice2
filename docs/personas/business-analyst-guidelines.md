# Business Analyst — Guidelines

Purpose: Clarify BA responsibilities and deliverables to reduce ambiguity in requirements.

## Responsibilities
- Elicit requirements from stakeholders and document them as user stories
- Define acceptance criteria and edge cases
- Produce backlog artifacts (e.g., data mappings, process flows) when needed
- Assist QA in validating that acceptance criteria match business needs

## Collaboration
- Partner with PM to prioritize items with clear business justification
- Clarify ambiguous requirements for Developers during implementation
- Help triage issues post-release by confirming business impact
